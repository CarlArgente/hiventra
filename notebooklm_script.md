## SCRIPT

Hiring the right person is hard. And the process itself is the problem.

**Weeks of wasted hours.** The average hire takes 36 days — and HR teams spend 70% of that time on manual screening, scheduling, and rewriting the same interview questions over and over.

**Every interview is different.** Without a structured process, two candidates for the same role get entirely different questions and scoring — making fair comparison nearly impossible.

**Resumes pile up, talent walks away.** When 80 resumes land in your inbox, most never get a proper look. The best candidates move on while you're still on page one.

**Not every applicant deserves an hour.** HR teams waste 14 hours a week conducting screening interviews — only to find out 10 minutes in that the candidate doesn't qualify.

**Hiventra** changes that.

Hiventra is an AI-powered talent intelligence platform built for modern hiring teams. At its core is **Carl** — an AI interviewer who does the heavy lifting for you.

Here's how it works.

Recruiters start by creating a job posting and uploading candidate resumes in bulk. Hiventra's AI instantly scores every resume — ranking candidates by skill match, experience, industry fit, and more — so your team knows who to focus on immediately.

Next, HR configures Carl for the role. Choose the interview mode — text, voice, or video. Set Carl's personality — friendly, professional, strict, or a technical deep dive. Pick the topics, the duration, and hit save.

Carl then conducts the actual interview with each candidate through their own portal. He asks questions, listens to answers, and when the interview ends, generates a full **Intelligence Report** — complete with a score, skill breakdown, key highlights, and risk flags.

Your hiring team reviews the reports, collaborates in real time, and moves candidates through a visual pipeline — from uploaded to hired.

Every AI decision is logged for full transparency. Every candidate sees exactly where they stand.

**Hiventra. Intelligent hiring, powered by Carl AI.**
