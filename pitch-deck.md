# Hiventra — Pitch Deck

---

## Slide 1: Title / Vision

**Hiventra**
*Hire Smarter. Move Faster. Never Miss the Right Person.*

An AI-native hiring platform that automates resume screening, conducts live AI interviews, and gives every stakeholder a purpose-built workflow — from recruiter to candidate.

---

## Slide 2: Problem

| Pain Point | Reality |
|---|---|
| Resume overload | 23 hours screening per hire on average |
| Scheduling delays | 5–7 days back-and-forth per interview |
| Inconsistent scoring | Different interviewers, different outcomes |
| No AI transparency | Opaque decisions, no audit trail |
| Disconnected teams | HR, managers, and dept heads in separate tools |
| Poor candidate experience | Weeks of silence, no status visibility |

**Result:** 44 days average time-to-hire. Top candidates accept elsewhere in 10.

---

## Slide 3: Solution

| Pain Point | Hiventra Fix |
|---|---|
| Resume overload | Bulk upload 50 resumes; Claude AI scores each instantly |
| Scheduling delays | Carl conducts live AI interviews autonomously — no scheduling |
| Inconsistent scoring | Standardized AI scoring with skill breakdown and risk flags |
| No AI transparency | Full AI decision log + on-demand explainability search |
| Disconnected teams | Unified collaboration: approval workflow, comments, comparison |
| Poor candidate experience | Candidate portal with real-time progress tracker and decision banner |
